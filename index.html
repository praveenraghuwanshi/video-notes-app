1. Core Mechanism (मुख्य प्रक्रिया)

Plants convert light energy from the sun into chemical energy stored in glucose molecules.

  • Light Reaction: Water splits into oxygen & hydrogen ions.
  • Dark Reaction: Carbon dioxide converts into sugar.
